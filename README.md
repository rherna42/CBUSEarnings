# CBUS Graduate Earnings

Interactive comparison of earnings four years after graduation for six MSU Denver College of Business bachelor's programs: Computer Information Systems, Economics, Finance, Accounting, Management, and Marketing.

Comparison groups: MSU Denver majors, other Colorado schools, major Colorado universities, the 13 CBUS peer institutions, urban-serving regionals, and all U.S. bachelor's programs.

Source: U.S. Department of Education program-level earnings data released for AHEAD negotiations. Pooled 2017-18 and 2018-19 completer cohorts, earnings in 2024 dollars.
