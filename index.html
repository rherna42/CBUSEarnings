Source. U.S. Department of Education program-level earnings released for AHEAD negotiations (file "1.5.26 OBBBA GE Data, HEA Group"). Pooled 2017-18 and 2018-19 completer cohorts. Earnings measured in CY2022 and CY2023, in 2024 dollars. Includes Title IV completers who are working and not enrolled four years after completion. Bachelor's programs only.
Same major. Programs with the same four-digit CIP code. Schools do not code majors the same way, so treat these comparisons as approximate. CIP 52.02 at MSU Denver is the Management program. For Computer Information Systems, "same major" combines three codes: 52.12 (Management Information Systems), 11.01 (Computer and Information Sciences, General) and 11.04 (Information Science/Studies). Schools file CIS and MIS programs under all three. Code 11.01 also includes some general computing and IT programs. Computer Science (11.07) is excluded.
Benchmark. Most programs are tested against the median earnings of high school graduates in their own state. Colorado's is $39,174, one of the higher state benchmarks. "Above state HS median" subtracts each program's own benchmark, which partly adjusts for state wage levels.
Caveats. Cohorts are small for some programs (Economics has 22 completers). MSU Denver is excluded from every outside group. Percentiles read as "higher than X% of programs in the group." Very large groups show a sample of 2,500 dots; percentiles use every program. Values below $20,000 or above $150,000 are drawn at the chart edge.
